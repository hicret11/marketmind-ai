## Definition

Paid media is any channel where you pay directly for reach or placement — search ads, social ads, display, paid influencer placements — as opposed to organic/earned reach.

## Why it matters

Paid media gives control and speed that organic channels don't: you can reach a defined audience today, not after months of building an audience. That control comes at a direct, measurable cost, which makes disciplined measurement essential.

:::key-idea
Paid media doesn't create demand from nothing — it accelerates and captures demand that already exists (search intent) or interrupts attention to create new awareness (social/display). Know which job a given channel is doing.
:::

## How it works

Cross-channel principles:

- **Funnel matching** — cold audiences need awareness-style creative; warm/retargeting audiences need direct-response creative.
- **Retargeting** — nearly always the highest-efficiency segment across any paid channel, since intent is already proven.
- **Budget pacing** — enough spend per test to reach statistical confidence, not spread paper-thin across too many campaigns.
- **Attribution** — understanding which channel/touch actually drove the result, even imperfectly.

## Framework

1. Separate budget into prospecting (cold) and retargeting (warm) — don't let one starve the other.
2. Start with one channel deep before spreading thin across many.
3. Define a target cost-per-result before launching, based on your margins/LTV.
4. Review weekly at first, then move to a steady cadence once patterns are clear.

:::example
A new business might allocate 70% of budget to cold prospecting and 30% to retargeting early on (building the retargeting pool), shifting toward more retargeting spend as that pool grows and proves more efficient.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Spreading a small budget across too many channels, so none gets enough signal to optimize.
- No retargeting setup at all — one of paid media's highest-leverage, lowest-effort wins.
- Chasing the lowest cost-per-click instead of the lowest cost-per-actual-result.

:::mistake
Comparing paid channels purely on cost-per-click without accounting for very different audience intent and funnel stage across channels.
:::

## Metrics to track

- Cost per acquisition/result, by channel and by cold vs. retargeting
- Return on ad spend (see CAC/LTV/ROAS/ROI)
- Frequency and audience saturation over time
