## Definition

The marketing funnel is a model of how a large group of unaware people narrows down, stage by stage, to a smaller group of paying customers. It's called a funnel because volume naturally drops at every stage.

## Why it matters

The funnel view stops you from over-focusing on one stage (usually traffic/awareness) while ignoring where volume is actually being lost. Most growth wins come from fixing the leakiest stage, not adding more people to the top.

:::key-idea
A funnel problem is rarely "not enough traffic." It's almost always a specific stage with an abnormally low pass-through rate — find that stage first.
:::

## How it works

A common structure (often called TOFU/MOFU/BOFU or AIDA):

- **Top (Awareness)** — the audience learns you exist.
- **Middle (Consideration)** — they evaluate you against alternatives.
- **Bottom (Decision/Conversion)** — they buy, book, or sign up.
- **Post-funnel (Retention)** — they come back or refer others.

## Framework

1. Define the funnel stages specific to your business (e.g. ad view → landing page visit → form fill → booking).
2. Measure the conversion rate between every consecutive stage.
3. Find the stage with the lowest relative pass-through rate — that's the priority.
4. Run one focused test to improve that stage before touching others.

:::example
If 10,000 people see an ad, 500 click through, but only 5 book a pilot call, the problem isn't the ad — it's the landing page or offer between click and booking.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Only tracking top-of-funnel metrics (impressions, reach) as if they equal business results.
- Building funnel stages that don't map to any real, measurable customer action.
- Trying to fix every stage simultaneously instead of the weakest one first.

:::mistake
Declaring a campaign a failure based on click-through rate alone, without looking at what happened after the click.
:::

## Metrics to track

- Conversion rate at each funnel stage
- Overall funnel conversion rate (top to bottom)
- Cost per result at the bottom of the funnel, not just cost per click at the top
