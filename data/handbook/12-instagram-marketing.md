## Definition

Instagram marketing is the deliberate use of Instagram's formats — Reels, feed posts, Stories, DMs — to build awareness, engagement and conversion for a brand or product.

## Why it matters

Instagram remains one of the highest-intent visual discovery platforms, especially for consumer, lifestyle and family-oriented products — a strong fit for visually emotional products like personalized experiences.

:::key-idea
Reels are Instagram's primary discovery engine; feed posts and Stories are better for depth and retention with people who already follow you.
:::

## How it works

- **Reels** — short, hook-driven video for reach beyond current followers.
- **Feed posts** — higher-production or evergreen content for profile credibility.
- **Stories** — casual, frequent updates and polls/questions for engagement and DMs.
- **DMs** — often the actual conversion point once trust is built via content.

## Framework

1. Hook in the first 1–2 seconds of every Reel — most drop-off happens immediately.
2. Post consistently rather than in bursts (2–4x/week is a reasonable baseline).
3. Use Stories to drive two-way interaction (polls, questions, "send us your story").
4. Turn DM conversations from content viewers into a defined next step (booking, link, offer).

:::example
A reaction-style Reel of a child hearing their personalized birthday song, ending with a clear caption CTA ("Get yours — link in bio"), combines emotional hook with a direct next step.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Treating Instagram as a portfolio (only polished, promotional posts) instead of a relationship channel.
- No clear call-to-action, leaving viewers unsure what to do next.
- Ignoring Stories and DMs, where a lot of real buying intent shows up.

:::mistake
Posting the same static image across Reels, feed and Stories without adapting to each format's native behavior — flat performance across the board is often the result.
:::

## Metrics to track

- Reach and plays on Reels vs. followers reached (discovery signal)
- Profile visits from content (intent signal, often stronger than likes)
- Story reply/DM rate
