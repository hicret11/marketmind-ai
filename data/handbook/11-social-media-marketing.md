## Definition

Social media marketing is building awareness, community and demand through organic content and interaction on social platforms — distinct from paid social advertising, though the two reinforce each other.

## Why it matters

Social platforms are where audiences already spend time and increasingly where they discover new products (including via search on the platform itself). A weak or generic social presence undercuts trust even for brands with strong ads.

:::key-idea
Every platform rewards different behavior — what works on one rarely transfers directly to another. Strategy has to be platform-specific, not just content reposted everywhere.
:::

## How it works

A social strategy generally balances three roles:

- **Discovery content** — designed to reach non-followers (trend-aware, shareable).
- **Community content** — designed to deepen relationships with existing followers.
- **Conversion content** — content that directly drives a click, DM or booking.

## Framework

1. Choose 1–2 primary platforms based on where your actual audience is, not where's trendy.
2. Define a weekly content mix across discovery / community / conversion.
3. Engage actively (comments, DMs, shares) — social is bidirectional, not a billboard.
4. Review platform analytics monthly and adjust format mix based on what performed.

:::example
A kids' party venue's audience is more likely on Instagram and Facebook (parents) than TikTok — while a teen-focused entertainment brand might see the opposite split.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Posting only promotional content with no community or discovery mix.
- Ignoring comments and DMs, which kills the platform's willingness to distribute your content further.
- Chasing every platform at once instead of doing 1–2 well.

:::mistake
Judging social success purely by follower count instead of engagement quality and downstream conversions.
:::

## Metrics to track

- Engagement rate (likes+comments+shares / reach)
- Follower growth rate relative to content volume
- Profile visits and link clicks as an intent signal
