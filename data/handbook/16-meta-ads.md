## Definition

Meta Ads covers paid advertising across Facebook and Instagram, run through Meta Ads Manager, using its targeting, bidding and optimization systems to reach specific audiences at scale.

## Why it matters

Meta's ad system is one of the most efficient ways to reach precisely defined audiences (by interest, behavior, lookalike, or retargeting) at controllable cost — but it heavily rewards good creative and clean testing structure, and heavily punishes sloppy setups.

:::key-idea
On Meta, creative is now the primary lever — targeting has gotten broader and more automated, so the ad that earns attention and action in the first 2 seconds usually wins over granular audience tweaking.
:::

## How it works

A campaign has three levels:

- **Campaign** — the objective (awareness, leads, sales, traffic).
- **Ad set** — the audience, budget and placement.
- **Ad** — the actual creative and copy.

**Retargeting** is showing ads specifically to people who already engaged (visited your site, watched a video, opened your app) — typically far cheaper and higher-converting than cold audiences, because intent is already established.

## Framework

1. Structure campaigns by objective, not by audience segment, when using broad/automated targeting.
2. Always run retargeting to warm audiences (site visitors, video viewers, engagers) alongside cold prospecting.
3. Test creative variations (hook, format, angle) more aggressively than audience variations.
4. Give each test enough budget/time to exit the learning phase before judging results.

:::example
A cold campaign to parents interested in birthdays, paired with a retargeting campaign to anyone who visited the site in the last 14 days but didn't buy — the retargeting audience usually converts at a much lower cost per result.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Judging an ad after a few hours or a tiny budget — Meta's algorithm needs data to optimize.
- No retargeting audience at all, leaving high-intent visitors to leave and forget.
- Overlapping ad sets targeting the same audience, causing internal competition and inflated costs.

:::mistake
Changing budget or creative constantly mid-test, which resets the learning phase and makes results impossible to compare cleanly.
:::

## Metrics to track

- Cost per result (lead, purchase) — the outcome metric, not just cost per click
- Click-through rate and hook rate (video) as creative-quality signals
- Frequency, to catch ad fatigue in retargeting audiences
