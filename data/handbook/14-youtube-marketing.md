## Definition

YouTube marketing uses long-form video and Shorts to build an audience through both search (YouTube is the second-largest search engine) and recommendation-driven discovery.

## Why it matters

Unlike most social feeds, YouTube content keeps generating views long after publishing — a well-optimized video can drive traffic for years, making it one of the highest-compounding content channels available.

:::key-idea
YouTube is part search engine, part entertainment platform — titles, thumbnails and descriptions matter as much as the content itself for getting discovered.
:::

## How it works

- **Shorts** — TikTok-style short-form for broad discovery and channel growth.
- **Long-form** — deeper content (tutorials, stories, behind-the-scenes) that builds loyal watch time and ranks in search.
- **Search optimization** — titles and descriptions matched to what people actually type in.

## Framework

1. Identify search terms your audience actually uses (e.g. "unique birthday gift ideas") and build content around them.
2. Use Shorts to drive channel discovery, long-form to build depth and trust.
3. Write titles and thumbnails that are honest but curiosity-driven — the click matters as much as the content.
4. Include a clear next step in the description and end screen.

:::example
A long-form "we surprised 5 kids with personalized birthday songs" video can rank for "unique birthday surprise ideas" for months, continuously bringing in new viewers.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Treating YouTube purely as a video hosting service with no SEO thinking.
- Weak thumbnails/titles that don't earn the click regardless of content quality.
- No consistent upload cadence, which hurts channel-level algorithmic trust.

:::mistake
Ignoring Shorts entirely — they're often the cheapest way to grow channel awareness that eventually feeds long-form watch time.
:::

## Metrics to track

- Click-through rate on thumbnails (search + suggested)
- Average view duration / retention curve
- Subscribers gained per video, not just total views
