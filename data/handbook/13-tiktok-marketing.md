## Definition

TikTok marketing is creating short-form, native-feeling video content optimized for TikTok's discovery algorithm, which prioritizes watch time and completion rate far more than follower count.

## Why it matters

TikTok can generate outsized reach for brand-new accounts with zero existing audience, purely from a strong video — a rare opportunity for smaller or newer businesses to compete with much bigger ones on attention.

:::key-idea
TikTok rewards content that feels native to the platform (raw, fast, sound-driven) far more than polished, ad-like content — "made for TikTok," not "repurposed from elsewhere."
:::

## How it works

Success factors on TikTok:

- **Hook** — first 1–3 seconds must earn the next second of attention.
- **Watch-through** — pacing and length matched to keep completion rate high.
- **Sound** — trending or emotionally fitting audio significantly affects distribution.
- **Native format** — text overlays, quick cuts, authentic tone over studio polish.

## Framework

1. Study 10–15 top-performing videos in your niche before posting your first one.
2. Post frequently (TikTok rewards volume and iteration more than most platforms).
3. Use trending sounds/formats where they genuinely fit the content — not forced.
4. Double down fast on whatever format starts outperforming your average.

:::example
A "guess what she said when she heard her name in the song" style reveal video fits TikTok's native reaction-content format far better than a produced brand ad would.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Repurposing polished ad creative directly from other platforms without adapting tone.
- Giving up after a few low-performing videos instead of iterating quickly.
- Ignoring the comment section, which strongly influences a video's distribution.

:::mistake
Overthinking production quality on TikTok at the expense of speed and volume — a rougher video that hooks well usually outperforms a polished one that doesn't.
:::

## Metrics to track

- Average watch time / completion rate
- Shares (a strong distribution signal on TikTok specifically)
- Follower growth per video, not just per week
