## Definition

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) is the systematic process of increasing the percentage of visitors who complete a desired action (purchase, booking, signup) — improving what you already have instead of only driving more traffic to it.

## Why it matters

Doubling your conversion rate has the same revenue effect as doubling your traffic — but is often far cheaper, since it works on demand you've already paid to attract.

:::key-idea
CRO is about removing friction and doubt, not adding persuasion tricks. Every point of confusion or hesitation on the path to conversion is a leak.
:::

## How it works

Conversion is shaped by:

- **Clarity** — does the visitor immediately understand the offer and next step?
- **Relevance** — does the page match what the ad/link promised?
- **Trust** — social proof, guarantees, professional design.
- **Friction** — form length, load speed, number of steps to complete the action.

## Framework

1. Identify the page/step with the biggest drop-off (use analytics, not guesses).
2. Form a specific hypothesis about why (e.g. "unclear pricing causes hesitation").
3. Test one meaningful change at a time where possible.
4. Measure against the actual conversion goal, not a proxy metric like time-on-page.

:::example
If a landing page's checkout step has a much higher exit rate than the pricing step, the issue is likely friction in checkout (too many fields, unclear pricing, no trust signal) rather than the offer itself.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Redesigning an entire page at once, making it impossible to know what caused a change in conversion.
- Optimizing for the wrong goal (e.g. clicks instead of completed purchases).
- Ignoring mobile experience, where most traffic often lands first.

:::mistake
Assuming low conversion means the offer is wrong, when often it's a specific, fixable friction point (page speed, unclear CTA, missing trust signal).
:::

## Metrics to track

- Conversion rate at each key step, not just overall
- Page load speed (a major, often-overlooked conversion factor)
- Drop-off rate on forms/checkout specifically
